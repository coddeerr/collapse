# Art Direction Exploration v0.1

Temporary direction name: 灯火边境 / Lantern Frontier

Core style: light 45-degree overhead, warm post-apocalyptic fairy-tale survival, hand-painted 2D game art.

Generated concepts:

- style-01-safe-zone-day.png: warm daytime safe-zone village.
- style-02-polluted-boundary-dusk.png: safe/unsafe boundary at dusk.
- style-03-night-attack.png: night monster attack / defense mood.

Design intent:

- The village must feel worth protecting.
- The unsafe zone must feel biologically wrong, not just dark.
- Keep the game readable for farming, building, paths, and defense lanes.
- Use warm safe light against cold mutated wilderness.
